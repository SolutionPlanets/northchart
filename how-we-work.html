How we work

Start with one hour. Fix one thing. Stay if it helps.

We begin by learning how your practice runs today, then fix the one thing that will make the day faster and cheaper to run. A longer partnership comes later, and only if it’s useful. You decide at every step.

Three stages

One front door, and a clear reason to take each next step.

Every stage is scoped in writing before work starts. Plenty of practices stop after the second one, and that’s a good outcome.

Stage 1 · Free · one hour

The workflow audit

We sit with whoever runs your front office and walk through a normal day: how patients get in, how the schedule fills, how work gets done, and how money comes back.

What you get
Written findings within 48 hours, ranked by time saved and cost. Yours to keep.
What we need from you
One hour with your practice administrator.
What you own
The findings, whether or not we work together.
Stage 2 · Where most practices start

A build that fixes one thing

We pick the single change from the audit that makes the biggest difference and put it in place: a tool set up, a workflow rebuilt, or something built for you.

What you get
One agreed scope, written down before work starts. Your staff trained on it.
What we need from you
A named contact, and access to the systems involved.
What you own
Your data and everything we build for you.
Stage 3 · Optional · later

Ongoing partnership

For practices that would rather not think about technology again. We keep what we’ve built running and take on the next gap, in the order that matters to you.

What you get
A technical person who knows your setup, and a roadmap you can see.
What we need from you
A short monthly check-in.
What you own
The roadmap, and the right to leave with your data.

What a partner owns

Technology decisions keep landing on someone whose job is running a clinic.

  • Your EHR vendor blames your internet provider, who blames your EHR vendor.
  • Nobody can say what you’re paying monthly across all your software.
  • A vendor is pitching an AI product and you have no way to judge it.
  • Reports you need every month take someone two hours to assemble by hand.
