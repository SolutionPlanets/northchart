Sound familiar?

Technology decisions keep landing on someone whose job is running a clinic.

  • Your EHR vendor blames your internet provider, who blames your EHR vendor.
  • Nobody can say what you're paying monthly across all your software.
  • A vendor is pitching an AI product and you have no way to judge it.
  • Reports you need every month take someone two hours to assemble by hand.
  • Your practice administrator has become the accidental IT department.
  • Contracts auto-renew because no one had time to review them.

None of these are emergencies. That's exactly why they persist — and why they quietly get expensive.

What we own

The work a practice technology partner actually does.

Not helpdesk. Not hardware. Our provider relationship management system first — then judgment, accountability, and the systems work in between.

Northchart PRM, then what's missing

It starts with our provider relationship management system: one clean record for every referring provider, every referral entered once, every call attempt logged, and the providers who have gone quiet flagged. After that — credentialing calendars, staff knowledge bases, the reports your EHR won't produce. Small tools, built to fit how your practice already works.

Vendor accountability

We become the point of contact for your EHR, billing, phone, and software vendors — so your staff stops chasing support tickets and someone technical is asking the hard questions on your behalf.

Decisions before contracts

Before you sign anything — new platform, AI tool, add-on module — we evaluate it, price out alternatives, and tell you plainly whether it's worth it. A three-year contract you didn't need costs far more than the review.

A roadmap you can see

A written, prioritized list of what to fix next, so technology stops being a series of surprises and starts being something you plan around like anything else in the practice.
