The problem

Referral leakage is invisible — until the schedule thins out.

Most specialty practices can't answer a simple question: which referring providers have gone quiet this quarter? Referrals arrive by fax, phone, and portal; nobody tracks the pattern; and a pediatrician who quietly starts sending patients elsewhere is noticed months later, as an unexplained dip in volume.

Every quiet referrer is revenue leaving without a phone call, a complaint, or a warning.
  • No one owns the referral relationship.

    The front desk logs the patient and billing codes the visit — but nobody is watching the referrer.

  • Reports go back late, or not at all.

    Slow follow-up is a leading reason referring providers quietly switch specialists.

  • Your EHR won't tell you.

    EHRs track patients, not relationships. The referral pattern lives in nobody's dashboard.
